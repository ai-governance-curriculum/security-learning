# Resources — mod-109-ai-governance-and-compliance-engineering

Primary sources for every claim in chapters 01–06 and the
five exercises. Prefer the official standard, the regulator's
text, the lab's own published policy, and the project's own
documentation over secondary summaries. Governance frameworks,
regulations, and lab policies all revise frequently — verify
versions, article numbers, clause identifiers, and threshold
definitions at time of reading. Where a resource is behind a
paywall (ISO/BSI/ANSI, AICPA), the primary text is the
authoritative source; preview materials and official summaries
are useful but not substitutes for a certification engagement.

## Caveats

- The AI RMF, ISO 42001, EU AI Act, SOC 2 TSC, SR 11-7,
  FDA GMLP/PCCP, HIPAA Security Rule, and the frontier-lab
  frameworks all evolve. Verify every citation at time of
  use.
- No link above substitutes for the organisation's General
  Counsel, DPO, Model Risk Officer, Clinical Affairs lead,
  Information Security Officer, or an accredited certifier.
  This resource list is reading support; compliance,
  certification, and regulatory determinations belong to
  accountable humans.
- Frontier-lab frameworks (RSP, Preparedness, FSF) are
  published commitments by the labs; they are revised and
  re-released. Treat specific threshold numbers and
  capability-category definitions as point-in-time
  statements that need re-checking before quoting.
- Where a resource is behind a paywall (ISO, BSI, ANSI,
  AICPA, IEEE), the paywalled text is the authoritative
  one for certification purposes; previews and summaries
  on vendor sites are useful but non-authoritative.
